Security & Trust

Your Health Data is Protected

Precision Medicine requires trust. Here is how Caedrix AI earns it.

🔒
HIPAA Compliant

All health data is handled under full HIPAA compliance, with signed Business Associate Agreements with every data partner. Your PHI is encrypted at rest and in transit.

🎛️
You Control Your Data

You decide what Caedrix AI can see. Grant access to specific providers, revoke at any time. Your data is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose other than your health guidance.

🩻
Not Medical Advice

Caedrix AI is your health coach and information partner — not your physician. Everything Caedrix AI shares is intended to help you ask better questions and understand your health, in partnership with your care team.
