Step-by-Step

Connecting Your Records

Four simple steps. Takes about five minutes. Works with virtually every major health system in the United States.

1
Getting Started
Create Your HealthEx Account

HealthEx is the secure medical record platform that Salus AI uses to access your health data. Creating an account is free and takes under two minutes. Your records are encrypted end-to-end and you control exactly what is shared.

Create Free Account → Learn About HealthEx
🔒 HealthEx is a HIPAA-compliant medical records platform. Salus AI has a signed Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with HealthEx — your health information is protected under federal law.
2
Connect Your Providers
Link Your Medical Providers & Insurers

Inside your HealthEx account, search for your doctors, hospitals, labs, and insurance carriers. HealthEx connects to over 21,000 providers nationwide — including Epic, Cerner, athenahealth, Kaiser, and most major health systems. Once linked, your records sync automatically.

Link a Provider →
💡 You can link multiple providers — primary care, specialists, hospitals, and labs. The more records connected, the more complete the picture Salus AI can provide.
3
Authorize Access
Grant Salus AI Access to Your Records

Once your providers are linked in HealthEx, authorize Salus AI to read your health data. You choose exactly what Salus AI can see — and you can revoke access at any time. This one-time authorization activates your Precision Medicine tier.

Authorize Salus AI →
🛡️ Salus AI reads your records to provide personalized guidance. We never sell, share, or use your health data for any purpose other than powering your health coach. Full details in our Privacy Policy.
