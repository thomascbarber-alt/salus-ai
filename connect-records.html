3
Authorize Access
Grant Salus AI Access to Your Records

Once your providers are linked in HealthEx, authorize Salus AI to read your health data. You choose exactly what Salus AI can see — and you can revoke access at any time. This one-time authorization activates your Precision Medicine tier.

Developer Implementation Note
HealthEx OAuth 2.0 + PKCE Flow — this button requires a dynamic server-side authorization request.

STEP A — Register Salus AI as OAuth client (one-time):
  POST https://api.healthex.io/oauth/register
  { "client_name": "Salus AI", "redirect_uris": ["https://salusai.health/auth/healthex/callback"],
    "scope": "patient/*.read offline_access" }

STEP B — Backend route /auth/healthex:
  1. Generate code_verifier = base64url(32 random bytes)
  2. code_challenge = base64url(SHA256(code_verifier))
  3. Redirect → https://api.healthex.io/oauth/authorize?...

STEP C — Callback /auth/healthex/callback:
  Exchange code for tokens, store encrypted (HIPAA).

Docs: https://docs.healthex.io/mcp-server/oauth-flow
🛡️ Salus AI reads your records to provide personalized guidance. We never sell, share, or use your health data for any purpose other than powering your health coach. Full details in our Privacy Policy.
